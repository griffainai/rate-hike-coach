# Mid-Call Panic Protocol
## 60-second emergency checklist when the user is freezing on a live call

Read top to bottom. Do not skip steps. This runs while the call is live — the user has the coach open on a second screen or phone, glancing at it between client sentences. No paragraphs. Clipped lines. Flight-deck cadence.

If the user is reading this, they are mid-call and they have already lost the script. That's fine. Recover.

---

## 00:00 — STOP

Stop typing. Stop reading email. Eyes on the call.

Do not speak for 3 seconds.

Three seconds is short on a clock and long in a conversation. It will feel like 10. It is fine.

---

## 00:03 — GROUND

Feet flat. Both feet. Floor.
Exhale slowly through the mouth. One time.
Hands off the keyboard. Hands visible (to yourself if remote, on the table if in person).

Do not start a sentence yet.

---

## 00:08 — LOCATE THE MANTRA

Look at the Post-it / notebook / tablet from Gate 5 of pre-call.

Read it once, silently.

If you cannot find it: read it from `sessions/[today]-pre-call-[client-name].md` → GATE 5 MANTRA.

---

## 00:15 — THE TIME-BUY LINE

Pick ONE. Say it. Do not improvise.

**If the client just made an emotional appeal (GRATITUDE / RELATIONSHIP):**
> *"I hear you. Let me take a beat on that and respond properly."*

**If the client just threw a competing quote / market threat:**
> *"Can you walk me through how you arrived at that number?"*
(This is the one question that buys time — they'll talk for 60 seconds, you'll recover.)

**If the client deferred to "let's talk next quarter":**
> *"I want to make sure we're aligned on timing — when you say 'next quarter,' are you talking effective date, or talking conversation date?"*

**If the client demanded justification:**
> *"My rate reflects the current scope and the current market. I'm happy to go deeper if you want, but that's the short version."*
(Then stop. Wait.)

**If you don't know what category they hit:**
> *"Can you say more about that?"*
(Universal time-buy. Costs nothing. Buys 45 seconds.)

---

## 00:25 — WALK-AWAY CHECK

While the client talks, glance at GATE 1 of `sessions/[today]-pre-call.md`.

Your walk-away number: $[X]
Current offer on the table: $[Y]

Is Y >= X?
- YES → proceed. Hold the number. Do not negotiate further down.
- NO → you are at the walk-away line. Go to 00:40.

---

## 00:40 — IF AT WALK-AWAY

The client has matched or gone below your walk-away. You said in pre-call you would end the call without an agreement at this number. Do that now.

Use one of these. Verbatim. Do not soften.

> *"It sounds like we're not aligned on the rate. I don't want to negotiate down from where I started — that wouldn't be fair to either of us. Let me think about it on my end, and let's both decide if this engagement is the right fit going forward."*

> *"I appreciate the directness. I'm not going to come down from $X. If that's not workable for you, I understand. Want me to send a written follow-up, or do you want to wrap up here?"*

> *"I think we should pause here. I'm not going to take the lower number, and I don't want to pressure you. Let's both step away for 48 hours and reconnect."*

**Do not apologize.**
**Do not over-explain.**
**Do not promise to "find a way to make it work."**

The walk-away is the walk-away. You committed to it 24 hours ago when you were thinking clearly. Trust that version of you.

---

## 00:50 — IF HOLDING THE LINE

If you're holding the rate and the client is still in the conversation, your job is to **say less, not more.**

Rules for the next 5 minutes:
- Answer in under 25 words
- Do not add new justifications
- Do not introduce concessions they didn't ask for
- Do not say "I understand" more than twice (it reads as caving)
- Let silences sit. Do not fill them.

If you feel yourself about to over-explain: bite the inside of your cheek. Physical interrupt.

---

## 01:00 — RESUME

You're back in the conversation. Continue.

If you slip again, restart this checklist from 00:00. As many times as needed.

---

## DO NOT DO THIS LIST

- Do not say *"let me think about it and get back to you"* unless you are above the walk-away and genuinely need to. This is the most common cope. It almost always means "I'm going to cave by email in 48 hours."
- Do not offer a *"compromise"* number that wasn't pre-approved during pre-call. Mid-call compromise = unilateral discount you did not authorize.
- Do not bring up *grandfathering*, *partial discounts*, *free value*, or *the next year* (COPE-05, -10, -08). These will all sound smart in the moment. They are cope. They are why you needed this protocol.
- Do not over-thank. *"Thanks for being so understanding"* tells the client they did you a favor by hearing you state your rate. They didn't.

---

## AFTER THE CALL

Do not go to email. Do not draft a follow-up. Do not re-litigate it in your head.

Go to `post-call-debrief.md`. Run it within 60 minutes of the call ending. While it's fresh.

---

## ONE LINE TO HOLD IN MEMORY

If you forget everything else:

**Say less. Hold the number. Let silence work for you.**
